SCHOOL LOCATIONS

Where are schools located?

Each point represents one school record. Many schools are clustered around Melbourne, while regional schools are more spread out. Select a sector or school type to compare their locations.

READING SCHOOL COUNTS

School counts do not tell us school capacity.

A small rural school and a large suburban school each count as one. The enrolment charts below help show this difference in size. They do not tell us how many extra students a school could accept.

Throughout this page, “FTE” means full-time-equivalent enrolment. Two half-time enrolments add up to one FTE.

SCHOOLS AND POPULATION

How do school numbers compare with population?

Large LGAs do not necessarily have large populations. The first map shows schools per 1,000 residents aged 5–19. The circle map shows how many residents in this age group live in each LGA.

residents aged 5–19 and 68 mainstream physical school records

That is 0.85 schools per 1,000 residents aged 5–19. Local schools report 64,621 FTE enrolments. These measures describe different things: where young people live and where students enrol.

What these comparisons can tell us

School locations, enrolments and population give different information. A low number of schools per resident does not necessarily mean a shortage. To investigate that, we would also need information about school capacity and where students travel from.

ABOUT THE DATA

Data sources and limitations

Official sources

  1. Victorian Department of Education: School Locations 2025 and February 2025 FTE enrolments. CC BY 4.0.

  3. Department of Education: Schools and enrolments 2026, historical school-count table.

How the data was used

Schools are joined by sector and school number. LGA names are matched to ABS codes. Ages 5–19 are the sum of three published age bands; they are an approximate youth denominator, not an exact school-age population.

“Mainstream physical” excludes Special and Language school types and Virtual School Victoria: 2,178 records. School locations do not enumerate every campus. Two zero-enrolment records remain in school counts but are excluded from the size distribution.

Boundary geometry is simplified for display. FTE measures enrolment, not capacity. Read methodology and checks ↗
